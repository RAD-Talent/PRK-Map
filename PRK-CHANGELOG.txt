PRK MAP - the community map for Project Rubi-Ka (Anarchy Online 18.4)
Based on Atlas of Rubi-Ka v2.1.0 by Onack (2010), rebranded for PRK.
Maintained by Everkill (.everkill on Discord) - ping him with map
updates/requests. Include an in-game F9 "Pos:" line for new locations.

PRK-specific markers use a "PRK:" prefix. Style guide:
- Cyan diamond #5fd7e2 = permanent PRK additions (GMI color scheme)
- Gold #f2cc79 = temporary/placeholder content that will change later
Coordinate formula: Demoder PMV world bounds X[31022..49770] Z[24880..48996].
