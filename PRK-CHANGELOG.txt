PRK COMMUNITY MAP - based on Atlas of Rubi-Ka v2.1.0 (Onack, 2010)
Maintained by Dylan + Claude. PRK-specific markers use bright cyan
diamonds with "PRK:" prefix (colors from PRK's GMI interface).
Style: permanent PRK additions = cyan #5fd7e2; temporary/placeholder = gold #f2cc79.
Coordinate formula: Demoder PMV world bounds X[31022..49770] Z[24880..48996].
